Plant Care Guide

Watering

Water your plants when the top layer of soil feels dry, allowing excess water to drain away to prevent root rot.

Light Requirements

Place the terrarium in bright, indirect light so the plants receive enough energy without being scorched by direct sun.

Soil Care

Use well-draining soil and occasionally remove dead leaves or debris to keep the growing environment healthy.
